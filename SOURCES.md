# Exercise sources and adaptations

Reviewed November 21, 2026. This is an independent F3-inspired prototype, not an official F3 product. Instructions are written for this app; no official logo artwork is used.

## F3 terminology

- [F3 Nation Q101 — Counting Cadence](https://f3nation.com/q101/): the leader calls the first three beats and the PAX calls the repetition number on the fourth. Flutter Kicks use this four-count convention at the user’s request.

- [F3 Nation Exicon](https://codex.f3nation.com/exicon): original Al Gore, American Hammer, and shoulder-tap terminology (checked November 17).
- [F3 Marietta Lexicon & Exicon](https://www.f3marietta.com/glossary): Merkin = push-up, Side Straddle Hop = jumping jack, LBC = Little Baby Crunch. Its coupon entries describe cinder-block curls, rows, overhead presses, squats, lunges and deadlifts. The Flora entry also describes a loaded goblet-squat hold. “Block” in this app means one cinder block, also called a coupon in F3.

Block cards use the names used for individual coupon exercises in F3 sources. They are individual exercises, not multi-exercise F3 routines. No fixed block weight, F3 cadence, or official F3 dose is implied.

## App-specific decisions

The active catalog is now **F3 Classics: 22 bodyweight and 14 block exercises**. It is an editorial selection grounded in F3 references, not a measured popularity ranking or an official F3 deck. The default equipment remains Bodyweight. Random draws remain uniform within the selected equipment/favorites intersection, without category balancing.

The F3 Codex Exicon's expanded Essential F3 Exercises panel was checked in the live site on November 20, 2026. It names Merkin, Burpee, Squat, Plank, Lunge, Mountain Climber, Imperial Walker, American Hammer, and SSH. The app omits Imperial Walker and SSH to honor the no-warm-ups scope. [F3 Coastal Empire's common-exercise list](https://www.f3coastalempire.com/f3-common-terms/) and the Exicon support the additional core, push-up, and crawl choices.

The active bodyweight choices are Merkins, LBCs, American Hammers, Al Gore, Burpees, Squats, Reverse Lunges, Jump Squats, Chilcutts, Side Plank, Flutter Kicks, Freddie Mercury, Alternating Shoulder Taps, Hand-Release Merkins, Carolina Dry Docks, Mountain Climbers, Plank Jacks, Bear Crawl, Werkins, Crab Walk, Big Boy Sit-ups, and Peter Parkers. The block choices are Curls for the Girls, Bent Over Rows, Overhead Press, Thrusters, Goblet Squats, Deadlifts, Block Swings, Man Makers, Chest Press, Skull Crushers, Coupon Lunges, Rifle Carry, Murder Bunnies, and Block Merkins.

F3 Marietta's glossary explicitly identifies Chilcutts as an elbow plank, Carolina Dry Docks as a hips-high push-up, and Freddie Mercury as a bicycle sit-up; its coupon routines use Curls for the Girls and overhead presses. The current official Exicon was also checked for Werkin (Wide Merkin), Thrusters, Big Boy Sit Up (World War II Sit-up alias), and Peter Parker. Existing instructions were retained for equivalent renamed movements. New Big Boy Sit-up and Peter Parker instructions are written for this app with explicit single-rep counting, rather than copied source prose or group cadence.

Dedicated warm-ups and general-fitness extras are retired from the active catalog, including Boxer Shuffle, arm circles, high knees, butt kicks, step/front-clap jacks, inchworms, standing cross-knees, and block marching. The Exicon also tags Monkey Humpers as a warm-up, so it was not added. Some retained strength/core moves can be used during a warm-up by individual Qs; the catalog excludes the dedicated warm-up choices rather than claiming a movement has only one use.

The previous 76 exercises had 12 bodyweight movements in each of five categories, chosen for variety. That quota no longer applies. Of the original 76 movements, 27 are now active with their original IDs and 49 retired IDs remain recognized only for preference storage compatibility. Coupon Lunges restores the previous block-reverse-lunges ID, so any saved preference for that same movement applies again. Retired favorites/exclusions never re-enter Browse or draws, do not inflate visible counts, and are not silently erased when another preference changes. New entries use new IDs. Search includes F3 names, plain-English subtitles, and previous names/aliases.

Doses are modest app defaults, not source-prescribed training plans: stationary block moves use 5–10 reps; traveling block moves use a user-chosen distance. Counted defaults are rounded to the nearest multiple of five. The app does not prescribe a standard masonry-unit weight. Block steps use a secure two-hand grip, a controllable load, and controlled movement. General loaded-movement references use other weights; adapting their movement patterns to a block is our implementation choice, not an endorsement of cinder blocks by those organizations.

American Hammers use the documented heels-down modification. Push-up instructions allow knees down as a modification; the active catalog no longer has separate modification-only cards. Each card spells out its variation and counting convention.

Flutter Kicks use four-count F3 cadence at the user’s AO: each full cycle is one displayed rep. Other repeated movements use the single-count convention explained on their cards; alternating single-count movements state total reps and the per-side breakdown. At the user’s AO, Flutter Kicks are counted rather than timed, so all former timed cards now use counts or open distance. Flutter Kicks alternate on each cadence beat, with the group calling the rep number on beat four; the chosen range counts complete cycles. Each Mountain Climber knee drive is one rep. Al Gore and Chilcutts use a spoken count hold; Side Plank uses a spoken count on each side. A spoken count is called at the group’s pace, not measured in seconds. All traveling moves—Bear Crawl, Crab Walk, Rifle Carry, and Murder Bunnies—say “Pick your distance” and let the user choose a finish point. Reverse Lunges and Coupon Lunges return to the starting position after each lunge, so those stationary variations keep their rep counts. These counting conventions are app choices, not changes to the underlying F3 movements.

Users can override counted exercises with an inclusive rep range from 5 to 100 in Choose your deck. Both endpoints and all drawn counts must be multiples of five. These are user-selected counts, not source-prescribed training plans. Per-side counts apply on each side; alternating totals split across sides, with one extra on the starting side for an odd total. Default restores the bundled dose. The range also applies to spoken hold counts; every traveling move remains open distance with no numeric target.


## Expanded block classics — November 20, 2026

The block deck was expanded from 6 to 14 after the user identified missing coupon staples. These choices are supported by exercise references and actual F3 workouts; no numerical popularity ranking is claimed. All use one block and retain the no-warm-ups scope. Instructions below the exercise titles are original app prose, with explicit counting.

| Added card | Evidence and implementation choice |
| --- | --- |
| Block Swings | The official [F3 Exicon Coupon Kettle Bell Swing entry](https://codex.f3nation.com/exicon?entryId=exicon-1763415020203-coupon-kettle-bell-swing) describes a hip-driven swing to chest/shoulder height. The card caps it at chest height and uses both hands. Coupon Swings and Kettlebell Swings are search aliases. |
| Man Makers | The live [Exicon](https://codex.f3nation.com/exicon) has a Blockee definition (block-supported push-up, stand, overhead press) and a Manmaker Burpee alias Blockies. It separately describes a dumbbell Manmaker with rows. The app explicitly uses the one-block burpee/press version; Blockee/Blockees/Blockies all find this single card, avoiding duplicate draw entries. |
| Chest Press, Skull Crushers, Block Merkins | [F3 Gwinnett's coupon burnout workout](https://www.f3gwinnett.com/coupon-burnout-challenge-for-the-gloom-belt/) lists these movements and explains their forms. The app uses the floor chest press, standing overhead triceps extension, and push-up supported on a flat stable block. |
| Coupon Lunges | [F3 New Orleans' Extreme Couponing](https://www.f3nola.com/2026/09/11/extreme-couponing-from-space-cowboy/) includes coupon lunges. This card restores the app's existing alternating reverse-lunge variation and stable ID, with the block held at the chest; it counts total backward-lunge-and-return cycles, not pairs. |
| Rifle Carry | The same New Orleans workout includes rifle carries; [F3 Black Swamp](https://f3blackswamp.com/2026/01/30/nightmare-perrysburg-municipal-park-coconut/) explicitly identifies the cinderblock-overhead rifle position. The app lets the user pick a distance along a clear, level path, using controlled footsteps to reach the finish point. |
| Murder Bunnies | The live Exicon describes moving the block forward then hopping the feet to either side. The card spells out that sequence on level ground and repeats it toward a user-chosen finish point under “Pick your distance.” |
